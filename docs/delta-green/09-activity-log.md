# Character Activity and Notes: How to Use Them

*Phase 2a step 5b, built 2026-11-19. Design: `07-step5-design.md`. Decisions: DECISIONS.md #31
and #35. Access: `PERMISSIONS.md`.*

Open a character in **Characters** (or **GM Tools → NPCs**) and choose the **Activity** tab. It
replaces the old *History* tab: the history entries are still there, mixed in with everything else.

## What the Activity tab shows

Newest first, each entry with its real date and, where the in-game calendar has one, its
**in-game date**:

| Entry | Example |
|---|---|
| **Posts** by the character: what an NPC said with *Speak as NPC*, or what a PC's player posted while that PC was active | *Dr Hale spoke in Morgue: "The body was moved…"* |
| **Mentions**: posts that tag the character (`10-npc-tags.md`; alias tags for the GM only), and other posts containing the character's name as a whole word (so "Hale" does not match "Haley"). For the GM the aliases count too; players search the name only, because aliases are GM secrets | *Mentioned by Agent Reyes in Main* |
| **Notes** by the GM and players | *Note by Sam (as Agent Reyes): Has a limp* |
| **Changes**: status, role, profile and sheet changes (the old history) | *Status: Active → Missing* |

- The **All / Posts / Mentions / Notes / Changes** buttons filter the list.
- Posts keep their formatting (bold, lists, tags, spoilers, images); clicking a tag switches the
  panel to that character. Long posts start collapsed; **Show all** shows the rest. **Go to post** closes the panel and
  scrolls the chat to that post, switching room if needed.
- 50 posts load at a time; **Load older posts** fetches more.
- Everyone only ever sees posts they could already read in the chat: whispers to other people and
  rooms they aren't invited to are left out by the database.

## Notes

- Type in the note box at the top, choose **Who can see it** and an **in-game date** (it starts on
  the current campaign date; clear it for no date), then **Add note**.
- **Players** choose *Me and the GM* or *Everyone*. **The GM** chooses *GM only* or *Everyone*.
  "Everyone" means everyone who can see the character.
- A player's note belongs to the player and is labelled with the character they were posting as,
  e.g. *Sam (as Agent Reyes)*. They keep seeing it if that character retires.
- Writers can **Edit** their own notes. Writers and the GM can **Delete** them (with a check first).
- Players can add notes only to characters they can see (revealed ones and their own PCs).
- Archived campaigns are read-only.
